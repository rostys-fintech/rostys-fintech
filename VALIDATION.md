# Early Validation Log

Do not convert outreach, likes or polite comments into customer traction.

## Discovery questions

1. When liquidity becomes tight, what information do you need first to decide where to move funds from?
2. How do you currently estimate how long each liquidity source will take to become actually usable?
3. Where is the biggest uncertainty: balances, approvals, conversion, transfer, settlement, or something else?
4. After seeing Liquidity Clock: what feels genuinely useful and what feels artificial or unnecessary?
5. What would this need to show before you would trust it during a real liquidity event?
