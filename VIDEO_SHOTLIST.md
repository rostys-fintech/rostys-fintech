# Video Shot List

## Pitch video

1. Title card — Liquidity Clock / tagline (5 sec)
2. Founder on camera or clean voiceover + one visual (20 sec)
3. Problem visual: amount vs time (20 sec)
4. Product hero with Two Clocks (35 sec)
5. Solana execution proof visual (20 sec)
6. Market wedge / GTM (20 sec)
7. Vision + closing logo (10 sec)

## Technical demo

1. Timing Stress hero — 10:00 / 17:00 / −07:00
2. Route table — highlight Solana Reserve
3. Recommendation card — −07 → +14 projected
4. Click **Run Live Solana Proof**
5. Capture Prepare → Sign → Submit → Confirm → Complete
6. Pause on `CONFIRMED`, real signature / slot / execution time
7. Open the real Solana Explorer transaction
8. Return to app — +14:00 confirmed state
9. Before → Confirmed After close
10. Optional Custom Stress edge case

If the app returns `SIGNED / NOT BROADCAST`, do not use that take as the final chain-proof shot. The state should remain at −07:00 and the button should show **Retry Live Confirmation**.

## Recording quality gate

- 1440p preferred; 1080p acceptable.
- Browser zoom 100%.
- No notifications or personal tabs visible.
- No mainnet wallet balances.
- No unpublished academic files or tabs.
- Cursor movements slow and intentional.
- No long pauses while waiting for the chain; cut only dead time, not the proof itself.
- Final demo under 3:00.
